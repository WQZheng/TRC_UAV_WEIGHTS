Q2  DOES OPEN-LOOP ACCURACY ACCOUNT FOR THE DIVERGENCE?
    deployment-trained checkpoint ADE = 9.05 m
    under this planner ADE 20.90 m -> 56.5% and 1.84 m -> 56.0%,
    an accuracy span of about 11x with almost no separation in outcome, so ADE has very
    little discriminating power in this regime.
    The deployment-trained checkpoint's ADE lies inside that span,
    which rules out gross predictor failure as the explanation but
    CANNOT establish planner-specific co-adaptation: error geometry,
    temporal structure and optimisation quality remain uncontrolled.
    Report as 'not explained by ADE alone and consistent with
    planner-specific adaptation', never as proof.

    NOTE: the primary checkpoint is absolutely BETTER under the
    planner it was NOT trained on, so the data do NOT form a
    'each predictor is best under its own planner' diagonal. The
    defensible statement is an asymmetric interaction: the
    deployment planner attenuates differences between predictors,
    the training-time planner exposes them. Figures must not imply
