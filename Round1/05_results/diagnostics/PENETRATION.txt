READING GUIDE
 System safety : ALL conflict rate vs p (does equipping the fleet make
   the whole corridor safer, and is the relation monotone?).
 Externality   : UNEQUIPPED conflict rate vs p. If it falls as p rises,
   equipped aircraft confer a POSITIVE externality on unequipped ones.
 Throughput    : ALL / group throughput and delay vs p (is safety
   bought at a throughput cost, or are both improved?).
